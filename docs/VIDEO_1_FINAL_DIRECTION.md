# AVUNO VIDEO 1 — FINAL VISUAL DIRECTION

## 1. One-sentence concept
A dark, quiet, minimalist architectural space with deep physical texture, illuminated by a single controlled source of warm light.

## 2. Emotional purpose
To make the visitor subconsciously feel they have entered a private, expensive, and timeless physical vault—a safe place for introspection and memory.

## 3. Environment
A private museum or monumental gallery space after closing hours. It feels physically real but undefined in its exact location.

## 4. Architecture
Minimalist, brutalist, or modern monolithic. Smooth or textured concrete/plaster walls, sharp geometric edges, and subtle architectural depth. No visible windows (unless it's the source of the light shaft), no doors, no furniture.

## 5. Lighting
One dominant warm source (2700K–3500K) originating from an architectural opening, narrow slit, or indirect ceiling source. It should cast a clear but soft gradient of light across the physical texture of the room.

## 6. Camera
Strictly locked off, or moving so slowly (imperceptible micro-dolly) that it feels completely stable and grounded.

## 7. Movement
Extremely subtle environmental movement. The slow shifting of the light angle, the faintest dust caught in the beam, or simply the organic grain of the film. The architecture itself must remain physically stable.

## 8. Composition
* **Left 50–60%:** Quiet architectural negative space.
* **Center:** Subtle environmental depth.
* **Right 20–30%:** The primary warm light source / architectural highlight.
The eye naturally moves from darkness → architecture → warm light.

## 9. Negative Space
60–70% of the frame is deeply shadowed but **NOT pure crushed black**. Subtle architectural information (concrete texture, faint reflected light, structural lines) must remain visible in the shadows so it feels like a real room, not an unfinished website.

## 10. Color
* **Primary:** Deep charcoal, near-black, muted indigo.
* **Secondary:** Dark concrete / brown-black architectural tones.
* **Accent:** Soft warm ivory / amber (the light source).

## 11. Texture
Real physical texture is mandatory. We must see the grain of the concrete, the subtle imperfections in the plaster, and the natural film grain of the camera sensor. 

## 12. Atmosphere
Real air inside a real building. Dust is completely optional and should only be present if it looks microscopically natural. No fog, smoke, or glowing magical particles.

## 13. Things to avoid
AI-generated visuals, 3D renders, pure crushed #000000 blacks without detail, horror vibes, haunted houses, neon, HDR, cyberpunk, people, recognizable furniture (desks, projectors, bookshelves), rapid camera movement, pan, tilt.

## 14. Desktop composition
The 16:9 frame anchors the light source on the far right. The vast left side provides the heavily shadowed, textured canvas for the Hero typography and the UI.

## 15. Mobile composition
The 9:16 crop focuses on the transition zone. The light source remains barely visible on the far right edge, providing depth, while the center-left shadowed texture provides the vertical canvas for UI stacking.

## 16. What makes it feel premium
The absolute restraint. The reliance on real physical texture and masterful cinematic lighting rather than flashy VFX, particles, or aggressive motion. It looks like it was shot on ARRI Alexa by a luxury architectural photographer.

## 17. What makes it feel like Avuno
It is the visual embodiment of "A quiet place to remember." It doesn't scream for attention. It exists solely to provide a safe, beautiful, physical anchor for the digital memories that will float over it.

## 18. Exact footage characteristics to search for
Real camera footage (no 3D/AI), locked-off tripod, minimalist concrete/plaster interior, single warm light shaft, deep shadows retaining texture, horizontal composition with light on the right.
